## About

Skudo gives every website its own email address. Mail reaches the inbox you already use, and you can reply without exposing your real address. Free, funded by donations.

## Features

- A unique alias for every site
- Reply from the alias
- Switch off one alias without touching the others
- Tracking pixels and link trackers removed from forwarded mail
- Your own domain, or Skudo's
- Works with Gmail, Outlook, iCloud or your own server

## Security and Privacy

- **Sealed alias mapping.** The forwarding address is encrypted, so a copy of the database does not reveal where your mail goes.
- **Relayed, not archived.** Messages go straight through and nothing is archived.
- **OpenPGP.** Upload your key and mail is encrypted to it before it leaves Skudo.
- **No ads, no profiling.** No third-party scripts, fonts or analytics on the website.
- **Operated in Italy** and subject to the GDPR.

## Transparency

- **Warrant canary**, signed and renewed every 30 days
- **Transparency log** with a signed Merkle root
- **Quarterly transparency report**
- **Legal requests:** a published [review process](https://skudo.org/legal/requests/)
